WishWe gives you straightforward controls over who can see your profile and your events. Whether you want to share openly with a wide circle or keep things close to your direct friends, you can adjust your privacy settings at any time.

---

## Private Profile

The **Private Profile** toggle is the master switch for your account visibility.

- **Off (default):** Any WishWe user who finds your profile — through search or an invite link — can view your profile information and public events.
- **On:** Your profile and events are only visible to users you have accepted as friends. Anyone else who searches for your username sees only your name and avatar — nothing else.

To toggle your private profile setting, go to **Settings → Privacy** and switch **Private Profile** on or off.

<Note>
  Turning on Private Profile does **not** affect your existing friendships. All your current friends retain full access to your profile and events.
</Note>

---

## Event-Level Visibility

In addition to your account-wide privacy setting, each individual event has its own visibility setting that you choose when creating or editing it:

| Visibility | Who can see the event |
|---|---|
| **Friends Only** | Only your direct WishWe friends can see and access the event. |
| **Friends of Friends** | Your direct friends **and** their friends can see the event, broadening your potential guest list. |

You set the visibility when you create an event, and you can change it later by editing the event. Your account-wide privacy setting and the event's own visibility both apply — whichever is more restrictive takes precedence.

<Tip>
  Decide on your preferred default visibility before you start creating events. Setting it intentionally each time prevents events from being shared more (or less) widely than you intended.
</Tip>

---

## Who Can Find You

- **Search:** Any WishWe user can search for your username and see your name and avatar in results.
- **Full profile:** If your profile is **private**, only your friends can view your full profile details, bio, and events. Other users see a limited view.
- **Invite links:** If someone has your direct invite link, they can send you a friend request — but they still cannot see your private profile content until you accept them as a friend.

---

## Managing Your Friends List

Your privacy settings work together with your friends list. To review or remove friends, go to **Profile → Friends**. Removing a friend revokes their access to your private profile and friends-only events immediately.

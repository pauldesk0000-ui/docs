Your WishWe profile is how your friends recognize and find you on the platform. A complete profile — with a username, name, and avatar — makes it easy for people to search for you and accept your event invitations. Taking a few minutes to set it up now saves confusion later.

---

## Profile Fields

| Field | Required | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Username** | ✅ Yes | Unique across WishWe. Used in search results and your personal invite links. |
| **First name** | No | Displayed on your profile and in friend suggestions. |
| **Last name** | No | Shown alongside your first name on your profile. |
| **Bio** | No | A short description about yourself. |
| **City** | No | Helps friends with shared locations discover you. |
| **Date of birth** | No | Used for age-related features and birthday events. |
| **Gender** | No | Optional personal detail shown on your profile. |
| **Avatar** | No | Your profile photo. Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC, HEIF. Max size: 5 MB. |

---

## Upload a Profile Avatar

To add or change your profile photo:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Edit Profile">
    Navigate to your profile page and click **Edit Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap your avatar">
    Click on your current avatar (or the placeholder icon) to open the upload dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an image">
    Choose a photo from your device. WishWe accepts **JPEG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC, and HEIF** files up to **5 MB**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Crop and confirm">
    Use the in-browser cropping tool to frame your photo exactly how you like it, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Files larger than 5 MB will be rejected. If your upload fails, try reducing the image size before trying again.
</Warning>

## First-Time Setup: Onboarding Wizard

When you create a new WishWe account, the app guides you through a **profile setup wizard**. The wizard walks you through choosing a username, adding your name, and uploading an avatar — all before you reach your home feed. You can skip optional steps and return to complete them later from your profile page.

---

## How to Edit Your Profile

You can update your profile information at any time:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile page">
    Tap your avatar or username to open your profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Edit Profile">
    Select the **Edit Profile** button to open the profile editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your details">
    Make your changes to any field — name, bio, city, date of birth, gender, or avatar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save** to apply your updates immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choosing a Username

Your username is one of the most important parts of your WishWe profile:

- It appears in **invite links** you share with others (e.g., `wishwe.online/invite/yourname`).
- Friends and other users can **search for you by username**.
- It must be **unique** — no two accounts can share the same username.

Before you commit, use the **username availability checker** on the sign-up or edit screen to confirm your preferred name is free. Choose thoughtfully, as your username is tied to your invite links and how people recognize you.

## Change Your Password

If you signed up with email and want to update your password:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Edit Profile">
    Open your profile page and click **Edit Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Change Password">
    Scroll to the **Change Password** section within the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your current and new passwords">
    Provide your current password, then type and confirm your new password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** to update your password. Your new password takes effect immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you signed up with Google, you manage your password through your Google account. The Change Password option only appears for email-based accounts.
</Note>

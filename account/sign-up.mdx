Welcome to WishWe! Creating an account takes just a few moments. You can sign up with your email address or use Google to get started even faster.

## Choose Your Sign-Up Method

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Email">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Go to WishWe and open the sign-up page">
        Visit [wishwe.online](https://wishwe.online) and click **Sign Up**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your email address">
        Type in the email address you want to use for your account and click **Continue**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check your email for a verification code">
        WishWe sends a **6-digit code** to your inbox. The code is valid for **10 minutes**, and you have up to **5 attempts** to enter it correctly.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the verification code">
        Type the 6-digit code into the verification field on the sign-up screen and click **Verify**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create your password">
        Choose a strong password for your account, confirm it, and click **Create Account**. You're in!
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Google">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Click 'Continue with Google'">
        On the sign-up page at [wishwe.online](https://wishwe.online), click the **Continue with Google** button.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose your Google account">
        Select the Google account you want to link to WishWe. If prompted, grant the requested permissions.
      </Step>
      <Step title="You're signed in">
        WishWe creates your account and signs you in automatically — no password or verification code needed.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Reset Your Password

If you ever forget your password, you can reset it in seconds:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the sign-in page">
    Visit [wishwe.online](https://wishwe.online) and click **Sign In**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Forgot password'">
    Below the password field, click the **Forgot password?** link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    Provide the email address associated with your account and click **Send Code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the verification code">
    Check your inbox for a 6-digit OTP and enter it on the verification screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your new password">
    Choose and confirm your new password, then click **Save**. You can sign in with it right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Log Out

To log out of WishWe, open your **profile menu** (your avatar or initials in the top corner of the app) and select **Log Out**. Your session ends immediately.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I didn't receive my verification code">
    Try the following:

    1. **Check your spam or junk folder** — verification emails can occasionally be filtered.
    2. **Wait at least 60 seconds** before requesting a new code, as delivery can sometimes be delayed.
    3. **Use the Resend Code option** on the verification screen to send a fresh code to your inbox.

    If you still don't receive it, make sure you entered your email address correctly and try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My code expired">
    Verification codes are valid for **10 minutes** from the time they are sent. If yours has expired, return to the verification screen and click **Resend Code** to receive a new one. Enter the new code before it expires.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I forgot my password">
    Use the password reset flow: go to the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**, enter your email, verify the OTP code you receive, and set a new password. See [Reset Your Password](#reset-your-password) above for detailed steps.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

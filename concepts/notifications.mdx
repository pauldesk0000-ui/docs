WishWe keeps you in the loop so you never miss a moment that matters — whether that's a friend accepting your request, someone joining your Plan, or a reminder that tonight's event starts in 24 hours. Notifications arrive both inside the app and via email, giving you multiple ways to stay connected.

---

## In-App Notification Feed

Tap the **bell icon** in the navigation bar to open your notification feed. The feed shows a paginated list of all your recent notifications, with unread items clearly marked. An **unread count badge** appears on the bell icon whenever you have notifications you haven't seen yet.

You can manage your notifications directly from the feed:

- **Mark individual notifications as read** by tapping them or using the mark-as-read action
- **Mark all as read at once** with the "Mark all read" option at the top of the feed

---

## Notification Types

<Accordion title="Friend Request Received">
  You receive this notification when another WishWe user sends you a friend request. Tap the notification to view their profile and accept or decline.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Friend Request Accepted">
  You receive this notification when someone accepts a friend request you sent. You're now friends and can see each other's events based on your respective visibility settings.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Someone Joined Your Plan">
  You receive this notification when a friend (or friend of a friend) joins a Plan you created. It lets you track participation as your event fills up.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Someone Marked Interest in Your Wish">
  You receive this notification when a friend marks interest in a Wish you created. Use this as a signal to gauge whether it's time to convert the Wish into a Plan.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A Wish Was Converted to a Plan">
  If you've marked interest in someone else's Wish and the creator converts it to a Plan, you receive this notification. Tap it to see the event date, time, and capacity, and join before spots fill up.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Event Start Reminder (24 Hours Before)">
  WishWe automatically sends you a reminder 24 hours before a Plan you've joined (or created) is scheduled to start. You don't need to set this up manually — it's scheduled for you.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Event Updated">
  You receive this notification when the creator of a Plan or Wish you're participating in makes changes to the event details (such as the date, time, location, or description). Always check the updated details so you're not caught off guard.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Event Cancelled">
  You receive this notification when the creator cancels a Plan or Wish you joined or marked interest in. The event is removed from your upcoming events list.
</Accordion>

## Email Notifications

In addition to in-app notifications, WishWe sends email notifications for key activities so you stay informed even when you're not actively using the app. Email notifications are sent for:

- Incoming friend requests
- Friend request acceptances
- Someone joining your Plan
- Someone marking interest in your Wish
- Plan reminders (24 hours before start)

---

## Managing Notifications

You can keep your notification feed tidy without missing anything important:

- **Unread badge** — The bell icon in the navigation bar shows a count of unread notifications at a glance.
- **Mark as read** — Tap any notification to open it; it's automatically marked as read. You can also mark it read without opening the linked content.
- **Mark all as read** — Use the "Mark all read" button at the top of your notification feed to clear the unread count in one tap.

---

<Tip>
  The 24-hour start reminder is scheduled automatically — you don't need to configure anything. It's set when a Plan is created and also when a Wish is converted to a Plan, so participants who were following the Wish get the same heads-up as those who joined after the conversion.
</Tip>

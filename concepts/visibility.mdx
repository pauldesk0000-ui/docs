Every event you create on WishWe has a **visibility setting** that determines who can see it in their social feed. You're in full control — whether you want to keep things tight with your closest circle or open the invite to a wider network.

## Visibility Options

### Friends Only

When you set an event to **Friends Only**, only your direct WishWe friends see it in their feed. Nobody outside your immediate friend list can discover the event through normal browsing — it simply won't appear for them.

This is the right choice when you're planning something personal or when you only want responses from people you already know well.

### Friends of Friends

When you set an event to **Friends of Friends (f-o-f)**, your direct friends see the event *and* so does anyone who is a friend of one of your friends. This broader reach is great for:

- Filling a Plan that needs more participants than your immediate circle can provide
- Discovering and connecting with new people through shared interests
- Spreading the word about community-style events

Friends of friends can mark interest or join just like your direct friends can, subject to the event's capacity and status.

---

## Share Links

Any event — regardless of its visibility setting — can generate a **shareable link**. Share links use a unique UUID token and can be sent to anyone via messages, email, or social media.

Anyone with the link can **preview the event**, even if they're not your friend and even if the event is set to Friends Only. This makes share links a powerful tool for targeted invitations outside your WishWe network.

<Note>
  Visibility settings control who sees your event in the WishWe social feed. Share links bypass friend-based visibility entirely — anyone with the link can preview the event regardless of whether they're in your friend network. Keep this in mind when deciding whether to share a link publicly.
</Note>

### Regenerating and Revoking Share Links

You have full control over your event's share link at any time:

- **Regenerate** — Creates a new share link with a fresh UUID token. The old link immediately becomes invalid, so anyone who had the previous link will no longer be able to use it.
- **Revoke** — Removes the share link entirely. The event can no longer be accessed via a direct link until you generate a new one.

Both options are available from the event's settings menu.

## Visibility and Private Profiles

If your WishWe profile is set to **private**, your events follow the same access rules. Friends of friends won't be able to see your events even if you select the Friends of Friends visibility option — your profile privacy takes precedence. Direct friends can still see and interact with your events normally.

---

## How to Set Visibility

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the event form">
    Start creating a new Wish or Plan, or open an existing event and tap **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Visibility field">
    Scroll to the **Visibility** section of the form. You'll see a dropdown with your two options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your setting">
    Select **Friends Only** or **Friends of Friends** depending on how widely you'd like the event to appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Tap **Create** (for new events) or **Save** (for edits). Your visibility setting takes effect immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

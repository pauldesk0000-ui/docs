WishWe organizes your social life around two distinct event types: **Wishes** and **Plans**. Wishes let you float an idea and gauge interest before committing to anything, while Plans lock in the details so your crew can show up. Understanding the difference helps you get the most out of every event you create.

## Wishes

A Wish is an aspirational event — something you'd love to do with friends, but without a fixed date or time attached. Think of it as raising your hand and saying "who's in for this someday?"

**When to use a Wish:**
- You have a fun idea but haven't nailed down the details yet
- You want to see how many friends are interested before committing
- You're planning something far in the future and the date is still uncertain

When you create a Wish, WishWe automatically adds you as **interested**, so you're always counted among the people who want it to happen. Your friends can then mark their own interest, giving you a clear picture of enthusiasm before you move forward.

### The "Interested" Status

Marking interest on a Wish is a lightweight, non-committal signal. It tells the creator — and everyone else who's interested — that you want this to happen. There's no date to hold open on your calendar, and no capacity to worry about. It's purely a show of intent.

### Converting a Wish to a Plan

Once you feel confident there's enough interest, you can **convert your Wish into a Plan**. Converting carries over the event details and notifies everyone who marked interest that a real plan is now in place. See the [Event Lifecycle](#event-lifecycle) section below for what happens at each stage.

---

## Plans

A Plan is a concrete event with everything nailed down: a date, a time, and a maximum number of participants. When you create a Plan, WishWe automatically joins you as a participant — your spot is reserved from the moment the event exists.

**A Plan includes:**
- **Event date** — the calendar day the event takes place
- **Event time** — the start time (used to calculate the end and schedule reminders)
- **Max participants** — the cap on how many people can join

### Joining a Plan

Your friends can join your Plan directly from their feed or from a share link. Each person who joins takes one spot from the available capacity. Once the last spot fills up, the Plan's status automatically changes to **closed**, preventing any further joins.

### Status Changes

A Plan moves through the following statuses automatically:

- **Active** — the Plan is open and accepting joins (or closed once full)
- **Closed** — the Plan has reached its maximum participant count; no new joins are accepted
- **Completed** — the Plan's event time has passed and a 5-minute grace period has elapsed

You don't need to do anything to trigger these changes — WishWe handles them for you.

## Wishes vs. Plans at a Glance

| Feature | Wish | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| Has fixed date | No | Yes |
| Has time | No | Yes |
| Has capacity limit | No | Yes |
| Participant action | Mark interest | Join |
| Can convert | Yes → becomes a Plan | — |
| Status changes | Active only | Active → Closed → Completed |

## Event Lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Active">
    The event is live and visible to friends (based on your visibility setting). For a Wish, friends mark interest. For a Plan, friends join up to the capacity limit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Closed (Plans only)">
    The Plan has reached its maximum participant count. No additional joins are accepted. Existing participants are still confirmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Completed (Plans only)">
    Five minutes after the Plan's scheduled end time, WishWe marks it as completed. The event moves out of your active feed and into your event history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Categories

When you create either a Wish or a Plan, you can assign it a **category** (for example, Food & Drink, Outdoors, Music, or Gaming). Categories help your friends quickly understand what kind of event you're proposing and make it easier to browse events in their feed.

---

## Cover Images

Adding a cover image makes your event stand out in the feed and gives friends a visual sense of what to expect. Cover images are optional, but recommended — especially for Plans you want to generate excitement around.

**Supported formats:** JPEG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC  
**Maximum file size:** 5 MB

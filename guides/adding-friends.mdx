Friends are at the heart of WishWe. When you connect with friends, their events appear in your feed and yours appear in theirs — so you never miss a wish, plan, or celebration that matters to you.

## Finding Friends by Username

If you already know someone's username, you can send them a friend request in just a few taps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Friends section">
    Tap the **Friends** icon in the bottom navigation bar to open your Friends hub.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for a username">
    Use the search bar at the top of the Friends section and type the person's username. Results appear as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open their profile">
    Tap on the person's name in the search results to open their profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Add Friend'">
    Tap the **Add Friend** button on their profile page to send them a friend request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens Next

Once you send a friend request, the other person receives a notification on WishWe. If they haven't responded after about 30 minutes, they'll also get an email reminder so your request doesn't get lost.

Your request will show as **Pending** on their profile until they respond.

## Accepting or Declining Friend Requests

When someone sends you a friend request, you'll find it waiting in your inbox.

1. Go to **Friends** in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap **Incoming Requests** to see all pending requests.
3. Tap **Accept** to confirm the friendship or **Decline** to pass.

## After Accepting

Once both of you are connected, you can each see the other's friends-only events in your respective feeds. You may also receive a short follow-up email welcoming your new friendship — a small nudge to check out what your new friend has planned.

## Removing a Friend

Changed your mind? You can remove a friend at any time without sending them a notification.

- Open their **profile**, or find them in your **Friends list**, then tap **Remove Friend**.

They won't be notified, and their friends-only events will no longer appear in your feed (nor yours in theirs).

## Viewing Mutual Friends

On any user's profile, you can see how many friends you have in common. This makes it easier to discover people you might know through your existing network.

<Tip>
  Want to connect with someone who isn't on WishWe yet? Share your personal invite link to bring them on board — a friend request is sent automatically when they sign up. See [Invite Links](/guides/invite-links) to learn how.
</Tip>

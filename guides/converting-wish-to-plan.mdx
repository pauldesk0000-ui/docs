One of WishWe's most powerful features is the ability to take a Wish — a casual expression of interest — and turn it into a fully scheduled Plan without losing any of the momentum you've built. Friends who already showed interest get notified automatically, so you don't have to chase anyone down.

## When to Convert

Convert a Wish to a Plan once you've gauged enough interest and you're ready to commit to a specific date and time. This is the natural next step when your Wish has attracted interest and you want to move from "who's keen?" to "it's happening."

## How to Convert a Wish into a Plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Wish">
    Navigate to the Wish you created. You can find it on your profile or in your activity feed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Convert to Plan'">
    On your Wish page, tap the **Convert to Plan** button. This option is only available to you as the creator of the Wish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a date and time">
    Select the date and start time for your event. This is the key piece of information that turns a Wish into a real Plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a max participants limit">
    Enter the maximum number of participants for your event. A minimum of **2** is required. WishWe uses this to automatically close the Plan once it fills up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the conversion">
    Tap **Confirm**. The Wish is now a Plan, and WishWe takes care of the rest automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens Automatically

The moment you confirm the conversion, WishWe:

- **Notifies every friend who marked interest** — each one receives an in-app notification and an email telling them the Wish is now a Plan, including the date and time.
- **Schedules a 24-hour reminder** — if the event is more than 24 hours away, all interested friends automatically receive a reminder notification 24 hours before it starts.
- **Updates the event type** — the event changes from Wish to Plan across the app, complete with date, time, and participant management.

## How Participation Changes

Friends who marked interest in the Wish are **not automatically joined** to the Plan. They receive a notification inviting them to officially join. Each interested friend needs to tap **Join** on the Plan to confirm their attendance. This keeps the participant list accurate and ensures only people who truly intend to come are counted.

<Note>
  Only the creator of a Wish can convert it to a Plan. If you're a participant who marked interest, you'll be notified when the creator makes the conversion — you won't see the Convert to Plan option yourself.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Before you convert, make sure your Wish has a location set. Friends who receive the notification will want to know exactly where to show up, and having that detail ready means they can make a decision to join right away without needing to ask.
</Tip>

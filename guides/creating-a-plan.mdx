A Plan is a fully scheduled event with a date, time, and location. Use it when you're ready to commit to the details and want friends to officially join. Once you post a Plan, WishWe tracks participants, manages capacity, and keeps everyone in the loop automatically.

## When to Create a Plan Directly

Create a Plan straight away when you already know the date, time, and place. If you're still figuring out whether there's enough interest or haven't settled on a date yet, start with a [Wish](/guides/creating-a-wish) instead — you can always convert it to a Plan later.

## How to Create a Plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open WishWe and start a new Plan">
    Go to [wishwe.online](https://wishwe.online) and click the **+** button, then select **New Plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a title">
    Give your Plan a clear title that tells friends exactly what's happening — for example, "Birthday dinner at Rosetta" or "Saturday morning football".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a description">
    Describe the event: what to expect, what to bring, dress code, or anything else friends need to know before joining.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date and time">
    Pick the date and start time for your event. WishWe uses this to schedule reminders and mark the Plan as Completed once it ends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a max participants limit">
    Enter the maximum number of participants for your event. A minimum of **2** is required. This cap lets WishWe automatically close the Plan when it fills up and reopen it if someone later leaves.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a location">
    Enter the address or place name. A clear location helps friends plan travel and reduces last-minute questions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a cover image (optional)">
    Add a photo to bring your Plan to life in the feed. Accepted formats: **JPEG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC, HEIF** — maximum file size **5 MB**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a category">
    Select the category that best describes your event to help friends find and filter Plans they'll love.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set visibility">
    Choose who can see your Plan:
    - **Friends Only** — visible only to your direct friends.
    - **Friends of Friends** — visible to your friends and their friends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Post">
    Hit **Post** to publish your Plan. It's immediately visible in your friends' feeds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After You Post

You're automatically **joined** to your own Plan as soon as you post it. Friends can see the Plan in their feed and join with a single tap. You'll receive an in-app notification and email each time someone joins.

## Capacity and the Closed Status

If you set a max participants limit, WishWe automatically marks your Plan as **Closed** the moment it reaches capacity — no manual action needed. Friends who try to join after that point will see the Plan is closed and won't be able to join. If a participant later leaves, the Plan reopens and becomes joinable again.

## Plan Status Lifecycle

| Status | When it applies |
|---|---|
| **Active** | After posting, while spots are available |
| **Closed** | When the Plan reaches max participants |
| **Completed** | 5 minutes after the event's end time |

## Editing a Plan

You can update your Plan's details — title, description, date, time, location, image, or visibility — any time before it closes. Tap **Edit** on your Plan to make changes.

## Deleting a Plan

Tap **Delete** on your Plan to remove it permanently. WishWe notifies every participant who has joined before the Plan is deleted.

<Warning>
  Deleting a Plan sends an immediate notification to everyone who joined. Make sure you're certain before confirming — this action cannot be undone.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  When in doubt, set a slightly generous capacity and adjust it downward later rather than capping it too low from the start. A Plan that fills instantly and closes can leave keen friends locked out — and reopening it requires a current participant to leave first.
</Tip>

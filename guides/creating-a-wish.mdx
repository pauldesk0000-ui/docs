A Wish is the lightest way to float an idea to your friends — no date, no commitment, just a spark. Whether you're dreaming of a spontaneous road trip or a casual dinner, posting a Wish lets you see who's keen before you lock anything in.

## When to Use a Wish vs. a Plan

Use a **Wish** when you don't have a date yet or you want to gauge interest before committing to specifics. Use a **Plan** when you already know the date, time, and place and are ready for friends to officially join. If you start with a Wish and interest picks up, you can always [convert it to a Plan](/guides/converting-wish-to-plan) later.

## How to Create a Wish

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open WishWe and start a new Wish">
    Go to [wishwe.online](https://wishwe.online) and click the **+** button, then select **New Wish**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a title">
    Give your Wish a clear, inviting title that answers the question: *What do you want to do?* Keep it short and specific — for example, "Weekend hike in the hills" or "Rooftop dinner this month".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a description">
    Use the description to share the vibe. Tell friends what you're thinking, why it would be fun, and any rough ideas you have in mind. The more colour you add, the more likely friends are to engage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a cover image (optional)">
    Add a photo that captures the mood of your Wish. Accepted formats: **JPEG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC** — maximum file size **5 MB**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a location (optional)">
    If you have a specific place in mind — even just a neighbourhood or city — add it here so friends can picture it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a category">
    Pick the category that best fits your Wish. This helps friends discover it and keeps your profile organised.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set visibility">
    Choose who can see your Wish:
    - **Friends Only** — visible only to your direct friends.
    - **Friends of Friends** — visible to your friends and their friends, great for growing the guest list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Post">
    Hit **Post** to share your Wish. It's now live in your friends' feeds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After You Post

You're automatically marked as **interested** in your own Wish the moment you post it. Friends will see your Wish in their feed and can signal their interest with a single tap — no sign-up friction, no commitment required.

## What Your Friends Can Do

Friends can mark interest in your Wish with a **single tap** on the Interested button. Every time someone marks interest, you receive an in-app notification so you can see who's in.

## Editing a Wish

Changed your mind on the details? Tap **Edit** on your Wish to update the title, description, cover image, or visibility at any time.

## Deleting a Wish

Tap **Delete** on your Wish to remove it permanently. Before it disappears, WishWe notifies everyone who marked interest so nobody is left wondering what happened.

<Tip>
  Adding a cover image makes your Wish pop in the feed and gives friends an instant visual feel for what you have in mind. Posts with images consistently attract more interest — it's worth the extra few seconds.
</Tip>

<Note>
  When you're ready to pick a date and make things official, you can convert your Wish into a Plan directly from the Wish page. All friends who marked interest will be notified automatically. See [Convert a Wish into a Scheduled Plan](/guides/converting-wish-to-plan) for full details.
</Note>

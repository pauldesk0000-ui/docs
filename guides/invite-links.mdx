Invite links are the best way to bring people into WishWe who haven't signed up yet. Instead of asking them to search for you after joining, your invite link handles the connection automatically — the moment they create an account using your link, a friend request from them lands in your inbox.

## What Is an Invite Link?

Your invite link is a unique URL tied specifically to your WishWe account. When someone opens the link and signs up (or signs in), a friend request is automatically sent from them to you — no manual searching required on either side.

Invite links follow this format:

```
https://wishwe.online/invite/{your-token}
```

## Generating Your Invite Link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your profile">
    Tap your **profile avatar** or navigate to your profile page from the bottom navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the 'Invite Friends' option">
    Scroll down to find the **Invite Friends** option on your profile page and tap it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your unique invite link">
    Your personal invite link is displayed and automatically copied to your clipboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it anywhere">
    Paste and share your link via any messaging app, email, social media, or wherever your friends are most active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Link Expiry

Invite links are valid for **24 hours** from the moment they're generated. After that, the link expires and anyone who opens it will be prompted to ask you for a fresh one.

You can generate a new invite link at any time — just head back to the **Invite Friends** option on your profile.

## What Happens When Someone Uses Your Link

When a friend taps your invite link, here's what they experience:

1. They're taken to WishWe's sign-up or sign-in page.
2. They create an account (or log in if they already have one).
3. A friend request is automatically sent from them to you.
4. Once you accept, you're connected and can see each other's friends-only events.

<Tip>
  Sharing a Wish as a Plan? Drop your invite link in the announcement so new people can join WishWe and connect with you right away — no awkward "search for me" instructions needed.
</Tip>

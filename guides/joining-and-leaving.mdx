WishWe makes it easy to express interest, commit to Plans, and change your mind if something comes up. Here's everything you need to know about managing your participation in events.

## Joining a Plan

To join a Plan:

1. Find the Plan in your feed or open it via a share link.
2. Tap the **Join** button on the Plan page.

You're instantly confirmed as a participant, and the creator receives an in-app notification letting them know you've joined.

## Marking Interest in a Wish

To mark interest in a Wish:

1. Find the Wish in your feed or open it via a share link.
2. Tap the **Interested** button on the Wish page.

The creator receives an in-app notification when you mark interest. Your interest also counts toward the social signal that may encourage the creator to [convert the Wish into a Plan](/guides/converting-wish-to-plan).

## What the Creator Sees

Every time someone joins a Plan or marks interest in a Wish, the creator receives:

- An **in-app notification** with the participant's name.
- An **email** summarising the new join or interest.

This keeps creators in the loop without them having to check the app constantly.

## Capacity Limits

When a Plan has a max participants limit set, WishWe automatically marks it as **Closed** once the limit is reached. After that, no further joins are accepted — anyone who tries to join will see that the Plan is closed. If a current participant leaves, the Plan immediately reopens and becomes joinable again.

## Leaving an Event

To leave a Plan or remove your interest from a Wish, open the event and tap **Leave**.

- **Leaving a Wish** removes you from the interested list.
- **Leaving a Plan** removes you from the participant list. If the Plan was closed because it had reached max capacity, it **automatically reopens** the moment you leave, giving other friends the chance to join.

## Viewing Participants

Open any event to see the full participant list and the current participant count. This is visible to all friends who can see the event.

<Note>
  You cannot join a Plan that is **Closed** (full) or **Completed** (ended). A Closed Plan may reopen if a participant leaves, but a Completed Plan is permanently locked once the event has finished.
</Note>

<Tip>
  If you're on the waitlist-side of a full Plan, keep an eye on it — or ask your friends to. If someone leaves, the Plan reopens instantly and you can jump in. Leaving a full Plan is a considerate move if your plans change, since it immediately frees up a spot for someone else.
</Tip>

Share links let your events travel beyond your friend circle. Whether you're spreading the word about an upcoming plan or giving someone a sneak peek at a wish, a single link is all you need — no WishWe account required to preview it.

## What Is a Share Link?

Each event you create can have its own unique shareable URL. Anyone who has the link can preview the event's details — including the title, description, cover image, and date and time — even if they're not your friend on WishWe.

Share links follow this format:

```
https://wishwe.online/share/{unique-token}
```

## Generating a Share Link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the event you created">
    Navigate to the event from your profile or feed and open its detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the Share button">
    Tap the **Share** button on the event detail page. If no share link exists yet, WishWe generates one instantly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your link is copied to your clipboard">
    The unique share link is automatically copied to your clipboard and ready to paste anywhere.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it anywhere">
    Paste the link into a message, email, social post, or anywhere else you'd like to spread the word.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Your Share Link

You stay in control of your event's share link at any time from the event's detail page.

- **Regenerate**: generates a brand-new token for the event. Use this if you want to refresh the link or limit who can access it going forward.
- **Revoke**: removes the share link entirely. Once revoked, the event is no longer publicly previewable — anyone who opens the old link will see an error.

<Warning>
  Regenerating a share link immediately invalidates the previous one. If you've already shared the old link — in a group chat, on social media, or anywhere else — those links will stop working. Make sure to redistribute the new link everywhere it was shared.
</Warning>

## Cover Images and Link Previews

Events with a cover image look their best when shared. When you paste a share link into most messaging apps or social platforms, the cover image appears automatically in the link preview — giving your event an eye-catching first impression before anyone even taps the link.

## Copying a Wish

When you view someone else's Wish through a share link, you'll see a **Copy Wish** button. Tapping it creates your own version of that Wish — same idea, your own event. It's a great way to get inspired by what others are planning and make it your own.

<Note>
  Share links provide a **preview only**. To interact with an event — such as RSVPing, commenting, or joining a plan — the viewer needs a WishWe account and must fall within the event's visibility settings (for example, being a friend of the creator for friends-only events).
</Note>

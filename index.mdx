## How WishWe Works

WishWe keeps things simple. You start with an idea — a hike, a dinner, a weekend trip — and share it with your friends. From there, it's easy to gauge interest, pick a time, and make it official.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Sign up at [wishwe.online](https://wishwe.online) with your email or Google account. Complete your profile so friends can find you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post a Wish or Plan">
    Have a vague idea? Create a **Wish** and let friends mark their interest. Ready to commit to a date? Create a **Plan** with a time and capacity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your friend network">
    Search for friends by username, send friend requests, or share your personal invite link with people who aren't on WishWe yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it happen">
    When the time is right, convert a Wish into a Plan. Interested friends get notified automatically, and everyone can join with a single tap.
  </Step>
</Steps>

WishWe is a social event-planning app at [wishwe.online](https://wishwe.online) that bridges the gap between "we should hang out sometime" and actually making it happen. Whether you're dreaming up a future adventure or organizing a concrete get-together, WishWe gives you the tools to plan events, rally your friends, and make memories in real life.

## What Is WishWe?

WishWe takes the friction out of social planning. Instead of bouncing between group chats, spreadsheets, and calendar invites, you get one place to capture ideas, coordinate with your friends network, and manage every detail of your events — from a spontaneous dinner to a meticulously planned weekend trip.

## The Two Building Blocks

Everything in WishWe revolves around two core concepts: **Wishes** and **Plans**. Understanding the difference helps you get the most out of the app.

### Wishes

A **Wish** is an aspirational event idea with no fixed date. Think of it as a social bookmark — a way to say *"I'd love to do this someday"* and share that intention with your friends. Wishes are low-commitment, low-pressure, and perfect for capturing inspiration before the details are locked in. You can turn any Wish into a Plan the moment you're ready to commit to a date.

### Plans

A **Plan** is a concrete event with a set date, time, and maximum number of participants. When you're ready to move from dreaming to doing, a Plan is how you make it official. You can invite specific friends, control who can see it, and manage attendance — all in one place.

Getting up and running with WishWe on your phone takes just a few minutes. Follow the steps below to download the app, sign in, and start connecting with your friends.

## Set Up WishWe on Your Device

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the app">
    Search for **WishWe** in the **App Store** (iPhone) or **Google Play** (Android), or tap the download link from [wishwe.online](https://wishwe.online). Install the app on your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open WishWe">
    Launch the app after installation. You'll land on the sign-in screen, where you can sign in to your existing account or create a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in or create an account">
    Choose how you want to get started:

    - **Existing account** — enter your email address and password to sign in immediately.
    - **New account via email** — enter your email address, check your inbox for a 6-digit verification code, enter the code, then set your password.
    - **Google** — tap **Continue with Google** to sign in or register instantly using your Google account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    If you haven't set up your profile yet, you'll be guided through a short onboarding flow. Choose a unique nickname, enter your first and last name, and optionally upload a profile photo so your friends can find and recognize you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're in!">
    Your home feed is ready. You'll see wishes and plans from the friends you're connected with. Start exploring, creating wishes, and planning events.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sign-In Methods

WishWe supports three ways to sign in on mobile:

| Method | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Email & Password** | Enter the email and password linked to your WishWe account |
| **Email (new account)** | Enter your email → receive a 6-digit code → verify and set a password |
| **Google** | Tap **Continue with Google** to authenticate via your Google account |

## Uploading a Profile Photo

Tap your avatar on your profile screen to upload a new photo. WishWe will prompt you to grant **photo library access** so it can read images from your camera roll. To change this permission later, go to your device's **Settings → WishWe → Photos**.

## Navigating the App

The WishWe mobile app is organized into a few key areas:

- **Home** — your friends' activity feed, showing the latest wishes and plans
- **Profile** — your personal profile page, where you can view and edit your details and settings
- **Auth screens** — sign-in and registration flows, accessible before you log in

<Tip>
  WishWe stays in sync between the web and mobile app. You can switch between [wishwe.online](https://wishwe.online) and the mobile app at any time — your data, friends, and activity are always up to date on both.
</Tip>

<Note>
  WishWe is designed for **portrait orientation**. For the best experience, keep your device upright while using the app.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't log in on mobile">
    Double-check that you're using the email address and password associated with your WishWe account. If you've forgotten your password, visit [wishwe.online](https://wishwe.online) on a browser, tap **Forgot password**, and follow the reset flow. Once you've reset it on the web, use your new password to sign in on mobile.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My feed is empty">
    Your feed shows activity from friends you're connected with. If your feed is empty, you may not have added any friends yet. Go to your profile and use the friend search to find people you know — once they accept, their wishes and plans will appear in your feed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Photo upload isn't working">
    WishWe needs permission to access your photo library to upload a profile picture. If you denied the permission when first prompted, you can re-enable it by going to your device's **Settings → WishWe → Photos** and setting access to **Read & Write** (iOS) or **Allow** (Android). Then return to WishWe and try uploading again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

The WishWe mobile app lets you create wishes, plan events, and stay connected with your friends — all from your phone. Available on both iOS and Android, the app delivers the same core WishWe experience you know from the web, optimized for mobile.

## Available on iOS and Android

Download WishWe from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android) and get started in seconds. Your existing WishWe account works immediately — no separate registration required.

## Key Features on Mobile

The WishWe mobile app includes the same core features you use on the web:

- **Feed** — see the latest wishes and plans from your friends in real time
- **Wishes** — browse, create, and manage your wish list
- **Plans** — organize and join event plans with your friends
- **Friends** — find and connect with people you know
- **Notifications** — stay up to date on activity that involves you
- **Profile** — view and edit your personal profile and settings

## Your Account, Everywhere

Sign in with your existing WishWe account and everything is ready to go. Your friends, wishes, plans, and notifications are all synced across the web app at [wishwe.online](https://wishwe.online) and the mobile app — any change you make on one platform is immediately reflected on the other.

## App Permissions

The WishWe mobile app may request the following permission:

| Permission | Why it's needed |
|---|---|
| **Photo Library** | Required to upload or update your profile picture from your camera roll |

<Note>
  The WishWe mobile app uses your existing WishWe account — there is no separate mobile registration. If you already have an account on the web, just sign in with the same credentials on mobile and you're ready to go.
</Note>

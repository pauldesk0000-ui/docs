---

You're a few steps away from turning your event ideas into reality. This guide walks you through creating your account, setting up your profile, connecting with friends, and making your first Wish or Plan on WishWe.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Head to [wishwe.online](https://wishwe.online) and sign up — it's free.

    **Sign up with email:**
    1. Enter your email address and click **Continue**.
    2. Check your inbox for a **6-digit verification code** and enter it on the next screen.
    3. Set a secure password to complete your account creation.

    **Sign up with Google:**
    Click **Continue with Google** to authenticate instantly using your existing Google account — no password setup required.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete Your Profile">
    A complete profile makes it easier for friends to find and recognize you. Fill in the following:

    - **Username** — your unique handle on WishWe (others will search for you by this).
    - **Name** — your display name as it appears to friends.
    - **City** — helps surface locally relevant events.
    - **Bio** — a short line about yourself (optional but encouraged).
    - **Avatar** — upload a profile photo in **JPEG, PNG, WEBP, or HEIC** format (max **5 MB**). WishWe includes an in-browser cropping tool so you can frame your photo perfectly before saving.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find Your Friends">
    Your friends network is the heart of WishWe. There are two ways to connect:

    - **Search by username** — use the search bar to find people you know and send them a friend request.
    - **Share your invite link** — every account comes with a personal invite link. Share it via text, social media, or any messaging app to let friends add you directly.

    The more friends you connect with, the more you'll get out of every Wish and Plan you create.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Your First Wish">
    A Wish is a low-pressure way to float an idea and see who's interested — no date required.

    1. Tap or click **New Wish**.
    2. Add a **title** that captures the idea (e.g., "Rooftop dinner downtown").
    3. Write a **description** to give friends more context.
    4. Upload a **cover image** to make your Wish stand out (optional).
    5. Select a **category** that fits your event type.
    6. Choose a **visibility setting** — private, friends only, or public.
    7. Tap **Save** — your Wish is live and visible to the audience you chose.

    <Tip>
      You can convert any Wish into a Plan at any time. Once you're ready to commit to a date, open the Wish and select **Turn into Plan** — all your existing details carry over automatically.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Or Jump Straight to a Plan">
    Already know the date, time, and who you want there? Skip the Wish and go straight to a Plan.

    1. Tap or click **New Plan**.
    2. Fill in the **title** and **description**.
    3. Set the **date** and **time** for your event.
    4. Define the **maximum number of participants** so you never accidentally over-invite.
    5. Add a **cover image**, **category**, and **visibility setting** just like a Wish.
    6. Invite specific friends directly from your network.
    7. Tap **Save** — your Plan is confirmed and invites go out immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  WishWe is available on **iOS** (App Store) and **Android** (Google Play), so you can manage your Wishes, Plans, and friend requests on the go. Search for **WishWe** in your device's app store to download the free app.
</Note>
